Create Online Games is a free online game maker that lets you create games with no coding.

You start from a ready-to-play game template and customize it by changing its parameters. There are templates for platformers, top-down shooters, pinball, snake, mazes, tower defense, 3D racing, idle games and more.

It's free, and there is no login and nothing to download.
